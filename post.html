Designing an OU structure for a fake company

15 September 2026 Active Directory

Replace this with your own write-up. A structure that works well: what you set out to do, what you built, why you made the choices you did, and what went wrong.

The goal

Before creating any users, I wanted a layout that would make Group Policy easy to apply later. Policies link to OUs, so the structure decides what you can target.

The layout

yourcompany.local
├── Departments
│   ├── Sales
│   ├── IT
│   └── Management
├── Groups
├── Service Accounts
├── Servers
└── Admin Accounts

Why it looks like this

  • Departments hold normal user accounts, so I can apply different policies per team.
  • Admin Accounts are kept apart from everyday accounts, so admin logins can have stricter rules.
  • Service Accounts get their own OU so I can block interactive logon for them.

Creating it with PowerShell

New-ADOrganizationalUnit -Name "Departments" -Path "DC=yourcompany,DC=local"
New-ADOrganizationalUnit -Name "Sales" -Path "OU=Departments,DC=yourcompany,DC=local"
